What changed

1. Bug fix: receivers were playing running back

When a team's backup running backs were hurt, the sim's "give the backup a series" rotation brought in the receiver listed last on the RB depth chart, even with a healthy starter. About 15 carries per team per season went to receivers, or to the QB keeping a handoff. Now only another RB (or the fullback) comes in. A receiver still plays there as the emergency option when every back is hurt.

Result: running backs get 370 carries per team per season, exactly the real number.

2. Backups get a little less work

On teams that split carries, the starter now gets a bit more. True workhorse backs weren't affected.

How to read the leaderboards

The "real range" comes from only 5 real seasons, but each check plays 16 sim seasons. Even a perfect sim would put about 1 in 6 seasons above the best real one by chance. One 2,000-yard season in a run isn't a red flag; several would be. Records are judged by per-game pace (17 vs 16 games), as you said.

Looked into, not changed

  • Defense: too many 80-tackle, 12-sack and 20-QB-hit seasons. Real teams sign replacements and use about 32 defenders a season; a sim team uses its same ~24 all year. Should settle once in-season signings exist (Step 7 piece 6).
  • Top passers and #1 tight end a little low: the most pass-heavy sim teams throw less than real. That's what loosening the pass-heavy limit (below) will test.
  • Receiving backs: total volume is right. What's missing is a 3rd-down back (below).
  • Plays per game 61.3 vs 62.4: a small gap in clock and drive logic that touches every stat. Left for a supervised session.

3rd-down back: built

New spot on your Depth chart tab (Offense): 3rd-down back. Rated on pass blocking, hands and route running (3 parts each) plus elusiveness (1 part). He plays running back in passing situations: 3rd/4th and 4+, 2nd and 8+, the two-minute drill, and two scores down in the 4th quarter (85% of those snaps).

AI teams: the best backup takes the job if he's within 4 points of the starter at it (staffs keep the lead back fresh). Otherwise the starter keeps it. About 29% of AI teams use a different back. If your starter is also your 3rd-down back, nothing changes.
